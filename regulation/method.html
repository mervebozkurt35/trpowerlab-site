Method and sources

How the regulation timeline was researched, and what was not read

How entries were chosen

An entry is in the list if it changed one of: how prices are formed or settled, who may operate in the market, how generation is supported or connected, or whether a new kind of business (storage, charging, aggregation, unlicensed generation) is allowed. The aim of the first version is the main milestones, not every amendment. Amendments that were found but not read are listed at the end of the log below.

How an entry was checked

  1. Find it. Start from the EPİAŞ market handbook and from a search for the instrument’s title.
  2. Read a page that reproduces the text. The Resmî Gazete and mevzuat.gov.tr could not be opened from the research environment. The pages used instead are Alomaliye’s reproductions of the gazette text, the Ministry’s consolidated text of the Electricity Market Law, EPİAŞ and TEİAŞ pages, and law-firm notes.
  3. Mark it. verified means the title, number and date were read on one of those pages, and the effect column is limited to what was read. draft means the entry rests on the handbook, a news item or inference, or the text itself was not read.
  4. Keep the doubt. Where a source disagreed with another, or a link between two instruments was inferred, the source note says so.

Reading the source labels on the cards

Each card ends with the kind of source it was written from, linked where a page was read.

  • Law text, regulation text, consolidated text, decision text: read in a reproduction of the official text, or in a consolidated text supplied for this project.
  • EPİAŞ market handbook: the handbook built from the EPİAŞ training series. Dates and descriptions that come only from it were not checked against the gazette.
  • News report, law-firm note, trade press, legal commentary: secondary sources. Where a number comes only from these, the card says “press reports” or “according to”.
  • Strategy paper, Ministry copy: the paper is linked but was not opened here; its content is as summarised in a TEPAV note.
  • The cards before 2001 rest on one law-firm sector report and should be checked against a history of the state electricity authority.

What the limits are

  • Most dates are publication dates in the Resmî Gazete as shown on the reproduction; where only the adoption date was read, the date column says so.
  • A reproduction is not the official text. Dates and numbers should be checked against the gazette before they are cited.
  • Press reports are used for tariff steps and support prices that no reproduced text was found for; these are marked.
  • The “rests on” lines on the cards come from the parent column. Some links were inferred from dates and titles and are labelled as such.
