Which instrument rests on which

Laws sit at the top, the regulations issued under them below, and the decisions, tenders and market launches that apply them below those. Open a branch to see what is under it; each title leads to the dated entry on the timeline. The line under a title says how it relates to the one above it. Where a link between two instruments was inferred rather than read, the line says so.

How the instruments rank, with the Turkish terms

Turkish rules form a ladder. A rule lower on the ladder must rest on one above it and cannot contradict it. The list reads from the top. This is a reading guide for the timeline, not a legal text; how binding a communiqué or a procedure is depends on its own legal basis.

Rank English Turkish Who issues it Example in this section
1 Constitution Anayasa Constituent power (referendum) Article 124 lets public bodies issue regulations; the Constitutional Court (Anayasa Mahkemesi) checks laws against it
2 Ratified international agreement Uluslararası antlaşma President signs, Parliament approves Energy and interconnection agreements (none in the timeline yet)
3 Law Kanun Parliament (TBMM) Law 6446, the Electricity Market Law (Elektrik Piyasası Kanunu)
3 Decree law (until 2018) Kanun Hükmünde Kararname (KHK) Council of Ministers, with delegated authority KHK 703 (2018)
3 Presidential decree (from 2018) Cumhurbaşkanlığı Kararnamesi (CBK) President None in the timeline yet
4 Presidential decision (before 2018: Council of Ministers decision) Cumhurbaşkanı Kararı (before 2018: Bakanlar Kurulu Kararı, BKK) President (before 2018, the Council of Ministers) Decision 3453 on renewable support prices; BKK 2015/8317 on transition periods
5 Regulation Yönetmelik A ministry or public body, here mostly EPDK, to apply a law Balancing and Settlement Regulation (DUY), Licence Regulation
6 Communiqué Tebliğ The same bodies; details and methods under a regulation EPDK’s revenue regulation and tariff communiqués (not yet in the timeline)
6 Procedures and principles Usul ve Esaslar EPDK, adopted by board decision The price limits procedure under the DUY
7 Board decision Kurul Kararı EPDK Board (Enerji Piyasası Düzenleme Kurulu) Decision 8965-2, price limit decisions, free consumer limit decisions

Two kinds of board decision matter. A regulatory decision (düzenleyici işlem) sets a rule for the whole market, such as the price limits or the free consumer limit. An individual decision (bireysel işlem) applies to one company, such as granting a licence or a fine. Both are called a Kurul Kararı, so the timeline names the subject, not just the number.

Stages and states of a text

English Turkish What it means here
Bill Kanun teklifi A proposal to Parliament. Since 2018 it comes from members of parliament; the older term for a government bill is kanun tasarısı
Draft Taslak A text published for comment before adoption; EPDK usually asks for opinions (görüş) on regulation drafts
Adoption date Kabul tarihi The day Parliament passed a law
Publication Yayım Printing in the Official Gazette (Resmî Gazete, RG); the date most regulations are cited by
Duplicate issue Mükerrer An extra issue of the same gazette number, often used for urgent texts
Entry into force Yürürlüğe giriş The day the text applies, which can differ from publication
Amendment Değişiklik (also tadil) A text that changes another; the amended text keeps its number
Provisional article Geçici madde A transition rule inside a law or regulation
Additional article Ek madde An article added later, usually numbered with an extra mark
Consolidated text Güncel metin or birleştirilmiş metin The current text with all amendments included
First, old, latest version İlk, eski, son versiyon (or hal) EPDK’s labels for versions on its pages
Repealed Mülga No longer in force; EPDK keeps lists of repealed texts
Annulment İptal A court removes a text: the Constitutional Court for laws, the Council of State (Danıştay) for regulations
Stay of execution Yürütmenin durdurulması A court suspends a text while the case is pending
Legal basis Dayanak The article of a higher text that authorises a regulation
Licence, pre-licence Lisans, önlisans The permits EPDK grants; the pre-licence comes first
Legislation Mevzuat All of these texts together
