Institutions

Who is responsible for which activity, matched to the rules that give them the role

Each card gives the role as the sources describe it, and the basis for it.

Institutions

TBMM Grand National Assembly

Passes the laws everything else rests on.

Basis: Laws 4628, 5346, 5710, 6446 and the laws that amend them

CB Presidency

Sets support prices after 2020 and may raise the unlicensed generation limit by decision.

Basis: Law 5346 Art. 6; Law 6446 Art. 14 (as amended by Decree Law 703)

ETKB Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources

Ranks and opens renewable resource areas (YEKA) and runs the competitions; sets nuclear energy sales procedures; sets details of facility inspection.

Basis: YEKA Regulation 2016; Law 5710 Art. 4

EPDK Energy Market Regulatory Authority

Licenses market activities; issues the market regulations (licence, balancing and settlement, ancillary services, capacity mechanism, unlicensed generation, storage, charging, aggregation, tariffs); sets tariffs, the free consumer limit and the price limits; approves the capacity mechanism budget; decides the YEKDEM cost projection.

Basis: Law 4628; Law 6446; regulations listed

TEİAŞ Turkish Electricity Transmission Company

Operates the transmission system and the balancing power market; procures ancillary services; proposes the capacity mechanism budget; gives grid connection opinions; ENTSO-E observer member; allocates interconnection capacity.

Basis: Law 6446; Ancillary Services and Capacity Mechanism Regulations; TEİAŞ page for ENTSO-E

EPİAŞ Energy Markets Operator

Runs the day-ahead market, intraday market, forward market and imbalance settlement; issues YEK-G certificates; announces the total renewable support charge; operates the Transparency Platform; named operator of the emissions trading market.

Basis: Law 6446 Art. 11; EPİAŞ regulation 2015; YEK-G Regulation; Law 7552

EÜAŞ Electricity Generation Company (state)

State generation; mobile plants may take part in the capacity mechanism; export limit rule for generation licensees refers to it.

Basis: Law 4628 (split of TEAŞ); import export regulation

EDAŞ Distribution companies

Run distribution grids; connect and settle unlicensed plants in their region; investment refunds for connections; privatised 2008 to 2013.

Basis: Law 6446; Unlicensed Generation Regulation; Law 7257

ÖİB Privatisation Administration

Sold the distribution companies (the page read does not name the body).

Basis: Strategy paper 2004

Takasbank Central settlement institution

Holds collateral and carries out advance, invoice and assignment payments of market participants.

Basis: Handbook, module on financial transactions

İDB Climate Change Presidency

Establishes the emissions trading system, allocates allowances, oversees monitoring and verification.

Basis: Law 7552

BOTAŞ Petroleum Pipeline Corporation (gas)

Wholesale gas prices; the 2026 retail gas tariffs followed its wholesale prices.

Basis: News item, April 2026
