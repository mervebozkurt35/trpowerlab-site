Generation the real-time data does not see

Many small plants, mostly rooftop and other unlicensed solar, are not metered by the system operator in real time. Part of what they produce is used on the spot by the building or factory that owns them, so it never travels through the grid; the rest goes into the local distribution network. Either way it is missing from the generation data above, yet it lowers the demand that the rest of the power system has to serve.

Since 30 December 2020 real-time consumption includes the demand these plants meet. That gives a way to estimate them: consumption minus metered generation minus net imports. In the data this estimate is close to zero at night and follows the sun by day, which is what unmetered solar would do. It also picks up any other unmetered generation and metering differences, so it is an estimate, not a measurement. It cannot be built for earlier years, because consumption did not include these plants then.

In October 2026 the estimate was 2.7 TWh, or 10% of reported consumption, against 9% in October 2025. Over the last 12 complete months it ranged from 4% (January) to 14% (June): it follows the sun, not demand. As a check, EPİAŞ publishes its own series for unlicensed plants (“YEKDEM Lisanssız Üretim Miktarı”, the settlement feed-in values of unlicensed plants within YEKDEM), with a delay of some months. In the 57 months from January 2022 to September 2026 for which both exist, our estimate is between 88% and 117% of that series (median 100%). The estimate is therefore close to EPİAŞ’s own figure, and it is available as soon as consumption and generation are.

Notes

  • Source: EPİAŞ Şeffaflık Platformu real-time generation and real-time consumption, through eptr2. Data to 13 November 2026.
  • Coverage: generation from January 2014 (EPİAŞ added plants to the series during 2013), consumption from March 2016, cross-border flows from 2018.
  • Unmetered solar: real-time generation misses most small, unlicensed plants connected to distribution grids. We estimate their output as consumption minus metered generation minus net imports, from 30 December 2020, when the data shows consumption starting to include it (the difference is about zero before and 3 to 4% of consumption after). The estimate also picks up any other unmetered generation and measurement differences. It matches EPİAŞ’s series “YEKDEM Lisanssız Üretim Miktarı” (call ren-ul-gen, published with a delay) closely, so it is a timely stand-in for that series; the figure itself comes from EPİAŞ’s settlement data, not from a measurement of ours. That series is the electricity unlicensed plants feed into the grid. Electricity that a plant’s owner uses on site without it reaching the grid is not visible in these data, so we cannot say how large it is.
  • Revisions: real-time figures are operational data and are revised; final settlement data arrive weeks later.
