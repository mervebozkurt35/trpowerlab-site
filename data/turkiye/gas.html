The spot gas price

The spot gas market (organised by EPİAŞ) trades gas for delivery on the day it is traded and the day after. The reference price, GRF, is the volume-weighted average of its day-ahead and intraday matches. Prices are published in lira per 1,000 standard cubic metres (Sm³). We also show them per megawatt-hour of gas, in euros and in US dollars per MMBtu, the unit of international gas prices. The conversion to energy uses a gross calorific value of 9,155 kcal/Sm³ (1,000 Sm³ = 10.65 MWh), and euro and dollar values use the exchange rate implied by EPİAŞ’s own power prices for each day, so they differ slightly from EPİAŞ’s own conversions. Use the buttons to change the time step, the unit and the range.

The spot price peaked in September 2022 at 135 EUR/MWh (26,129 TL per 1,000 Sm³), 6.3 times the 2021 average. Over the last twelve months the daily price ranged between 13,721 and 22,738 TL per 1,000 Sm³, and the median change from one day to the next was 0.3%. One possible reason for such small moves is a link to BOTAŞ’s regulated tariff; we have not checked this. In euros the price also changes with the exchange rate.

Gas and power prices

Gas-fired plants often set the price of electricity in Türkiye, so the price of gas is a first guide to the price of power. The chart puts the monthly average day-ahead power price (PTF) next to the spot gas price, both in euros per megawatt-hour. Our data does not show which plant sets the price in each hour, so the chart shows how closely the two move together, not that gas sets the price. A power price above the gas price is expected: a gas plant needs more than one megawatt-hour of gas to produce one megawatt-hour of electricity, and has other costs. PTF is also cut off by the price cap and the floor at zero.

From September 2018 to October 2026 the monthly PTF and the spot gas price have a correlation of 0.94 (1 would mean they always move together); for month-to-month changes it is 0.63. Over the last twelve months alone it is -0.26. The long-run link is consistent with gas plants often setting the price, and the 2021 to 2023 rise is the clearest case, but the relationship is not stable: in May 2026 PTF averaged 11 EUR/MWh with gas at 31 EUR/MWh, when gas supplied 11% of generation. In months with a lot of hydro, wind and solar the gas price tells little about the power price, because other plants set it for many hours.

Gas-fired generation

The share of Türkiye’s electricity generated from natural gas. It usually falls when hydro, wind and solar output is high, and rises in dry years and in summer peaks.

Trading volume

The spot gas market handles a small part of Türkiye’s gas, most of which is bought under contracts. The chart shows how much gas was matched in the spot market each month, in millions of standard cubic metres.

The balancing gas price

Gas shippers who are out of balance with the gas system buy or sell the difference at the balancing gas price (BGP). In the data the BGP purchase price is a fixed percentage above the spot reference price and the sale price a fixed percentage below it (yearly medians of the purchase premium: 3% from 2018 to 2022, 5% from 2023). That looks like a rule written into the market design, not a price found by trading; this is still to be confirmed with EPİAŞ. The practical meaning is that being out of balance in the gas system costs a constant share of the gas price.
