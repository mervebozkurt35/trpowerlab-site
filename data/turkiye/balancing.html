What the instructions are

After the day-ahead and intraday markets close, the system operator (TEİAŞ) keeps supply and demand in balance in real time. When the system is short of energy it orders plants to produce more, an up-regulation instruction (YAL). When the system has too much it orders plants to produce less, a down-regulation instruction (YAT). Every instruction carries a label. According to the Balancing and Settlement Regulation, label 0 is for balancing energy, label 1 for relieving constraints in the transmission grid, and label 2 for ancillary services such as frequency control. We use the label published with each instruction; we have yet to confirm with EPİAŞ that the published code is this label.

The grid-constraint label matters for cost. A plant ordered up for a constraint is paid the price it offered, whatever the day-ahead price was. The share of instructions carrying label 1 therefore shows how much of balancing is about where electricity is produced, not about how much. Volumes below are the energy instructed; the bottom of the page shows how much was delivered.

Instructions by label

Bars above zero are up-regulation, bars below zero down-regulation, split by label. Warm colours are up, cool colours are down; darker means label 0.

The three months with the most up-regulation under the grid-constraint label were April to June 2026: the operator ordered 5,540 GWh up, 97% of it with that label, against 2,181 GWh and 6% a year earlier. Down-regulation was higher: 7,060 GWh against 1,144 GWh.

Share of instructions for grid constraints

From 2020 to 2025 the grid-constraint label covered between 1% and 19% of up-regulation in a year. The highest monthly share since 2020 was 98% in May 2026, far outside that range.

Net volume actually delivered

Delivered volume is what plants actually supplied after the instruction. Up-regulation delivered plus down-regulation delivered is the net: positive when more energy was added to the system than taken out, negative when the system was long on balance.
