Türkiye electricity market overview

The latest month and year so far, compared with a year earlier

Data through 13 November 2026. Updated automatically each morning from EPİAŞ (last check 14 November 2026, 13:56, Istanbul time).

Latest full month: October 2026 · data to 13 November 2026

Prices. October’s average PTF was 52.9 EUR/MWh (2,925 TL/MWh), up 7.8% on September and down 6.2% on October 2025 in euro terms. So far in 2026 PTF averages 39.4 EUR/MWh, down 33.3% on the same period of 2025. SMF averaged 2,655 TL/MWh, and the system was short in 47% of hours (63% a year earlier).

Demand. Consumption in October was 28.4 TWh, up 3.7% on October 2025 as reported and up 3.0% net of unmetered generation. Demand peaked at 45,410 MW on 21 October. Year to date, consumption is up 1.7% as reported and up 0.7% net of unmetered generation.

Generation. Renewables supplied 50% of metered generation in October, against 31% in October 2025. The biggest change from a year earlier was in hydro, +4.7 TWh (7.6 TWh against 2.8 TWh).

Markets and volatility. On an average October day the gap between the cheapest and the most expensive hour was 55.9 EUR/MWh, against 49.6 a year earlier. Bilateral contracts carried 65% of traded volume, the day-ahead market 32% and the intraday market 3.1%. The intraday average price (AOF) was 127 TL/MWh below PTF. PTF fell to zero in 18 hours (1 in October 2025).

November so far (1 to 13 November). PTF has averaged 54.4 EUR/MWh (3,040 TL/MWh), down 9.3% on the same days of 2025. Consumption is up 5.6% as reported, and renewables supplied 53% of metered generation against 28%. The month is not complete, so these figures are not comparable with the full-month figures above.
